Privacy Policy

Stocks Breakout Alerts · Last updated September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stocks Breakout Alerts ("we," "us," or "the service") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with its SMS text-message program. Stocks Breakout Alerts is a personal, non-commercial stock-screening notification service operated by an individual sole proprietor.

Information we collect

To deliver text-message alerts, we collect and store only the mobile phone number of the person who has opted in to receive them, together with a record of that consent and basic delivery logs (for example, whether a message was sent successfully). We do not collect names, financial account information, location data, or any other personal information through the SMS program.

How we use your information

The mobile phone number is used for one purpose only: to send the automated stock-screening alerts you have opted in to receive. We do not use it for marketing, advertising, or any unrelated purpose.

We never share or sell your information. No mobile information or opt-in data will be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties for any purpose other than the delivery of the messages you requested (for example, our messaging provider, which transmits the messages on our behalf).

The SMS program

  • Program name: Stocks Breakout Alerts.
  • Message content: automated notifications when stocks approach a computed technical breakout level, generated by a personal screening tool.
  • Message frequency: up to 1 message per day.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-in and consent

Enrollment is manual and self-initiated: a recipient is added to the program only when they have expressly provided their own mobile number and asked to receive these alerts. We do not purchase, rent, or import phone numbers, and we do not add anyone without their direct consent.

Opting out and getting help

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a single confirmation message and then stop sending texts; no further messages will be sent unless you re-enroll.

For help, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at the email address below. For questions about your carrier plan, contact your mobile carrier.

Data retention and security

We retain the opted-in mobile number and consent record only for as long as the number remains enrolled in the program. When a number opts out, its record is removed from the active send list. Data is stored using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a small personal service.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
